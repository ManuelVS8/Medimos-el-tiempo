Measuring historical time

To indicate when a past event occurred, we can distinguish between units of measurement for recent past and ancient past:

Measuring recent time
  • Minute: 60 seconds
  • Hour: 60 minutes
  • Day: 24 hours
  • Week: 7 days
  • Month: 30 days (28, 29, 31)
Measuring the past
  • Year: 365 days (366)
  • Lustrum: 5 years
  • Decade: 10 years
  • Century: 100 years
  • Millennium: 1000 years
